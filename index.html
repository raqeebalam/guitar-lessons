FAQ

I don't have a guitar yet. Can I still start?

Yes. The trial doesn't need one. Afterwards I'll recommend a good beginner acoustic for your budget, with links for Amazon, Guitar Center or Sweetwater.

How do online lessons work?

Live on Zoom with two camera angles so you see the fretboard and strumming hand clearly. You play along and get corrected in real time. Practice notes, chord sheets and backing tracks follow each lesson.

Which time zones do you teach in?

US and Canada evenings (Eastern, Central, Mountain and Pacific), UK and Europe afternoons and evenings, Gulf and Pakistan daytime. Tell me your time zone and I'll send available slots.

How do I pay?

By card or bank via a secure Payoneer payment link in USD, GBP or EUR. Students in Pakistan can pay by JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank transfer.

What ages do you teach?

10 and up, including complete adult beginners. Kids' lessons are shorter (45 min) and more song-based.

What if I miss a lesson?

Reschedule free with 24 hours' notice. The monthly package includes one make-up lesson.

Acoustic or electric?

Either. Beginners usually start on acoustic; if you already own an electric, we start there.
