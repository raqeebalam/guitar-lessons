FAQ

I don't have a guitar yet. Can I still start?

Yes, the trial doesn't need one. Afterwards I'll recommend a good beginner acoustic for your budget and country.

How do online lessons work?

Live on Zoom (or WhatsApp video). I show chords and strumming in close-up, you play along, and I correct in real time. You get practice notes and backing tracks after each lesson.

Which time zones do you teach in?

Pakistan, UK, Europe and the Gulf during my day; US and Canada in your evenings. Tell me your time zone and I'll send available slots.

How do I pay from outside Pakistan?

Wise or Payoneer (USD, GBP, EUR, AED) or direct bank transfer. Inside Pakistan: JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank transfer.

What ages do you teach?

10 and up. Kids' lessons are shorter (45 min) and more song-based.

What if I miss a lesson?

Reschedule free with 24 hours' notice. The monthly package includes one make-up lesson.
